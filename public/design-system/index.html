03 — Layout

A twelve-column grid with room to breathe.

  • Grid12 columns, 24px gutters. Section label on columns 1 to 3, headline from column 4.
  • Margins72px on desktop, 24px on phones. Beyond 1920px the content stays 1776px wide and centred.
  • Rhythm144 to 176px between sections on desktop, 96px on phones.
  • Breakpoints860px: grids stack, the Climb stops pinning, popups go full screen. 1921px and up: content width is capped.
  • Section labelA 32px champagne rule, then an uppercase label: "Available spaces".
  • ChaptersThe home page is one climb in thirteen chapters: ground, the harbour, the towers, the climb, in figures, smart building, spaces, landmark, services, in their words, location, arrival, enquire.
  • Floor gaugeFrom 1101px wide: a thin gauge in the left margin shows the floor (1 to 53) and the chapter you are in. It hides during the Climb, which has its own counter.
  • Pinned momentsThe manifesto (270vh), the Climb (420vh) and the spaces rail (horizontal) pin on desktop when motion is allowed. Phones and reduced motion get plain stacks and a swipeable rail.
  • Sail maskPhotos in the smart-building frame and beside the founder are cut with a curved top-left corner, echoing the towers.

05 — Motion

Slow, soft, and always optional.

  • HeadlinesWords rise through a mask, 1.1s, ease (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 45ms between words.
  • Body and cardsFade up 20px, 1s, staggered up to 450ms within a group.
  • HeroLooping film under the title. The title drifts at 16% of the scroll speed and fades out.
  • ManifestoPinned for 270vh. The sentence lights up word by word as you scroll; the buttons appear when it is complete.
  • Spaces railPinned. Scrolling slides six cards sideways; a champagne line shows progress.
  • Smart buildingThe list drives the picture: the item nearest the middle of the screen is lit and its photo fades in.
  • The ClimbPinned for 420vh. Floor 1 to 53 follows the scroll, four stops cross-fade for 1.1s, the photo pushes in from 110% to 103%.
  • NumbersCount up once, 1.8s, when 60% visible.
  • Popups0.35s fade, 0.55s rise. Escape, the Close button and the dark backdrop all close them. Arrow keys flip photos.
  • Desktop extrasSmooth scrolling, soft cursor ring and magnetic buttons. Fine pointers only.
  • Reduced motionEverything above is off: no film, no pin, no reveals. The Climb becomes four stacked stops.

06 — Quality rules

What every page has to pass.

  • Buttons and linksEvery one does something real. No empty links. Space cards open popups, enquiries open an email or WhatsApp with the message ready, phone numbers open WhatsApp, the address opens the map.
  • FormsLabels, inline errors announced to screen readers, focus moves to the first problem, a clear ready state, and the draft is kept until you start a new enquiry.
  • AccessibilitySkip link, visible focus ring, semantic landmarks, dialog semantics with focus return, full keyboard use, alt text, contrast as listed above.
  • PerformanceNo framework. Images load lazily, videos start only near the screen and pause when they leave, nothing loads for data saver or reduced motion.
  • Sharing and searchTitles, descriptions, social cards and structured data (Organization, Place). Currently set to noindex while this is a preview.
